# Sovereign Navigator  
### Remedy & Re‑Stabilization Matrix  
A Progressive Web App (PWA) built to guide an individual through a complete stabilization pathway — from unsheltered intake to full sovereign restoration — using the ceremonial, geometric, and aesthetic language of the SincerelySovereign system.

---

## ✦ Purpose  
The Sovereign Navigator provides a structured, ten‑gate remedy sequence that unifies institutional stabilization with sovereign restoration.  
It is designed for field use, outreach, intake, and personal recovery mapping.

---

## ✦ The Ten Gates  
Each Gate represents a critical stabilization milestone:

1. **Gate 0 — Status Gate**  
   Establish baseline standing and intake priority.

2. **Gate I — Unsheltered Intake**  
   Initial contact, vitals, and crisis stabilization.

3. **Gate II — Identity Gate**  
   Identity verification and sovereign name recognition.

4. **Gate III — HMIS Enrollment**  
   Entry into the Homeless Management Information System.

5. **Gate IV — Benefits Hub**  
   SNAP, SSI, Medicaid, and local provisioning.

6. **Gate V — Medical & Behavioral**  
   Physical and behavioral health triage.

7. **Gate VI — Housing Pathway**  
   Shelter routing and long‑term placement.

8. **Gate VII — Economic Stabilization**  
   Employment, income, and financial recovery.

9. **Gate VIII — Legal Stability**  
   Documentation, legal support, and covenant restoration.

10. **Gate IX — Sovereign Restoration**  
    Full reintegration, autonomy, and sovereign return.

## ✦ Technology  
- **HTML‑only PWA** (no framework, no build system)  
- **Inline CSS tokens** for full aesthetic control  
- **Inline SVG crest system**  
- **Service Worker (sw.js)** for offline capability  
- **manifest.json** for installable PWA behavior  
- **GitHub Pages** deployment
